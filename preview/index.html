Backups and privacy

Backups contain private memories and attachments. Keep them somewhere private. Cloud restore is not available in this beta. Time capsules are not encrypted.

Camera and microphone require browser permission. Use test memories until live testing is complete.

Speaking to write

For grown-ups: on-device speech recognition is required. If unavailable, Chromebook accessibility Dictation (Search + D) or Windows voice typing (Windows + H) may work; review device speech privacy settings first.
